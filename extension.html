Every call, in your toolbar.

See each appointment, ticket, transfer and callback the moment Sara handles it. A badge counts new calls, and a desktop notification tells you who called and why.

Download for Chrome Use the web dashboard

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Version 1.0.0, 36 KB.

Install in under a minute

Sara isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. It's four quick steps.

  1. Download and unzip

    Click Download for Chrome, then unzip sara-extension.zip into a folder you'll keep, such as Documents.

  2. Open your extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions in the address bar and switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

  3. Load Sara

    Click Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped. Sara appears in your extensions list.

  4. Pin and sign in

    Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, pin Sara, open it, and sign in with your business email. You'll get a 6-digit code.
