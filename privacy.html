Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This page explains what information Sara collects when you use this website or sign up for the service, why it's collected, and who it's shared with. It's written to be accurate to what actually happens — not boilerplate.

What we collect

When you sign up for the Starter plan or send a quick question through the forms on this site, we collect what you type into them: your name, email, company name, phone number, and (if provided) your own automation webhook URL. These forms are processed by our own automation (n8n) and saved to our private customer list. A copy is emailed to us so we can reply.

When you chat or talk with Sara on this website, your messages are sent to our own automation (n8n), which uses Google's Gemini AI to write her replies and generate her voice. If you talk out loud, your browser turns your speech into text before anything is sent to us; in some browsers (such as Chrome) that speech recognition is done by the browser maker's own service. Our automation keeps a short-term log of each conversation so we can fix problems and improve Sara's answers. Your conversation is also kept in your browser tab until you close it. If you book a test appointment, the name, phone number and time you give are saved to our private test calendar.

We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

How it's used

  • To set up and operate your Sara subscription
  • To respond to questions submitted through the contact form
  • To generate responses in the chat and voice features

We don't sell your information to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who it's shared with

  • Google Gemini — writes Sara's replies and generates her voice in the chat and voice assistant on this website
  • n8n (our automation platform) — receives the sign-up and question forms on this site, and stores and processes your business's call activity data (appointments, tickets, transfers, callbacks) if you're a subscribed customer

We don't share your information with any other third party.

Your call activity data

If you're a Sara customer, the appointments, tickets, transfers, and callbacks your business receives are stored in your own Google Sheet and made available to you through the private dashboard and Chrome extension. This data is not publicly visible and is not shown anywhere on this marketing site.

The customer dashboard and Chrome extension

Sara customers sign in to the dashboard and the Chrome extension with their email address and a 6-digit code we email them. Codes expire after 10 minutes. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of each code and sign-in session, never the code itself. A session lasts up to 30 days, and you can end it at any time by signing out. Your browser or extension keeps the session key on your own device. The dashboard and extension show only your own business's calls, and nobody else can see them. We limit how often codes can be requested to protect accounts.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your contact information or cancel your subscription at any time by emailing us. That includes any website chat conversations kept in our automation logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated directly to active subscribers by email.
