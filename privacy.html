Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Sara collects when you use this website or sign up for the service, why it's collected, and who it's shared with. It's written to be accurate to what actually happens — not boilerplate.

What we collect

When you sign up for the Starter plan or send a quick question through the forms on this site, we collect what you type into them: your name, email, company name, phone number, and (if provided) your own automation webhook URL. This is handled through Jotform, our forms provider.

When you chat or talk with Sara on this website, your messages (and, for voice, your spoken words) are processed by Jotform's AI Agent service to generate her replies. Jotform may keep a record of the conversation in our Jotform account so we can review and improve Sara's answers.

We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

How it's used

  • To set up and operate your Sara subscription
  • To respond to questions submitted through the contact form
  • To generate responses in the chat and voice features

We don't sell your information to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who it's shared with

  • Jotform — processes form submissions, payment collection (when connected), and the website chat and voice assistant
  • Jotform AI Agents — runs the chat and voice assistant on this website
  • n8n (self-hosted automation) — stores and processes your business's call activity data (appointments, tickets, transfers, callbacks) if you're a subscribed customer

We don't share your information with any other third party.

Your call activity data

If you're a Sara customer, the appointments, tickets, transfers, and callbacks your business receives are stored in your own Google Sheet and made available to you through the private dashboard and Chrome extension. This data is not publicly visible and is not shown anywhere on this marketing site.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your contact information or cancel your subscription at any time by emailing us. That includes any website chat conversations kept in our Jotform account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated directly to active subscribers by email.
