Tickets

Callbacks

If you can't take the call, she records when the caller wants to hear back.

What kinds of businesses is Sara for?

Businesses that live on the phone and book appointments: clinics, dental and beauty practices, salons, and home service companies.

Can callers still reach a real person?

Yes. When a caller asks for someone, Sara notes why and transfers them to the right person or department. If no one is available, she takes a callback request.

What does Sara actually connect to?

Sara runs on your own automation workflow and writes every appointment, ticket, transfer, and callback to your activity dashboard in real time.

Can I see the activity feed anywhere else?

Yes — the web dashboard and the Chrome extension both show the same live feed, so you can check in from your desk or your browser toolbar.
