Every call ends somewhere useful

When a caller hangs up, Sara has already sorted the call into one of four outcomes and written it to your dashboard. Nothing waits in a voicemail box.

Appointments

She checks your hours, offers open times and confirms the booking before the caller hangs up.

Example: Haircut and beard trim, today at 5 pm

Tickets

Problems are written down with the caller's details and a priority, so urgent ones stand out.

Example: AC unit not cooling, high priority

Transfers

When a caller needs a person, she notes why and passes them to the right desk.

Example: Asked for sales, passed to the team

Callbacks

If nobody can take the call, she records when the caller wants to hear back.

Example: Call back tomorrow at 3 pm
